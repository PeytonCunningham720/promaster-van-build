
# Ram ProMaster — Custom Van Conversion

**159" Wheelbase | Full Off-Grid Build**

This document walks through the build section by section. Photos for each section are in the corresponding folder.

---

## 🛋️ Living Space

→ [`Living Space/`](<./Living%20Space/>)

![Living Space](<./Living%20Space/main.jpg>)

The main living area sits behind the cab. It features a custom Baltic birch bench seat with custom couch cushions, a Lagun swivel table, and a dedicated chess table. Upper cabinetry with gas struts and grabber latches lines the walls above. The oak cieling, tongue-and-groove cedar wall panels, and Lonseal commercial-grade flooring give the interior a warm, finished feel. Dimmable puck lights run the length of the ceiling.

---

## 🍳 Kitchen

→ [`Kitchen/`](./Kitchen/)

![Kitchen](./Kitchen/main.jpg)

The kitchen is built around a Ramblewood 2-burner propane cooktop, fed by an undermount propane tank with a remote fill and a 12V Fuelock solenoid for safe shutoff. A Dometic NRX 115C 4 cu. ft. 12V/24V compressor refrigerator keeps food cold without drawing excessive power. The custom cabinetry is built from Baltic birch with a solid countertop block, soft-close drawer slides and hinges, locking drawer pulls, and a tip-out tray, all finished in Rubio Monocoat wood oil. A space-saving RV sink connects to the fresh water system.

## 🛏️ Bed

## ⚡ Electrical

→ [`Electrical/`](./Electrical/)

![Electrical](./Electrical/main.jpg)

The electrical system is built around a full **Victron Energy ecosystem**:

- **Batteries:** 300Ah LiFePO4 (3x 100Ah 12V)
- **Inverter:** Victron inverter/charger
- **Distribution:** Victron Lynx Distributor with MEGA fusing
- **Solar:** 400W (2x 200W monocrystalline panels) via Victron SmartSolar MPPT charge controller
- **Alternator charging:** Victron Orion-Tr Smart Isolated DC-DC charger
- **Monitoring:** Victron Cerbo GX hub + GX Touch 70 display + SmartShunt, with battery temperature and water level sensors
- **Shore power:** 30A/125V inlet for campground hookups
- **Panel:** Blue Sea Systems AC main panel, DC breakers, and 12-circuit blade fuse block
- **Outlets:** USB-C DC outlets and 15A GFCI outlets throughout
- **Controls:** 4-gang marine rocker switch panel with color-coded momentary buttons

## 💧 Plumbing

→ [`Plumbing/`](./Plumbing/)

![Plumbing](./Plumbing/main.jpg)

Fresh water is stored in a 39-gallon wheel-well mounted tank with a KUS level sensor that reports to the Victron display. The system includes a water pump, strainer, and accumulator for consistent pressurized flow, run through 1/2" PEX-A with SharkBite fittings and a ball-valve drain. A pipe heater provides freeze protection in cold climates. An external water inlet makes refilling easy without opening the van, and the sink drains through a flexible external drain hose, so no grey tank is needed.

---

## 🔥 Heater

→ [`Heater/`](./Heater/)

![Heater](./Heater/main.jpg)

An **Espar heater** provides reliable heat in any conditions. It draws from the van's fuel tank, uses minimal fuel, and is thermostatically controlled, making it ideal for cold-weather camping or full-time winter living. Complemented by full multilayer insulation: 3M Thinsulate SM600L acoustic/thermal, Low-E reflective radiant barrier, Kilmat sound deadening, and XPS foam board under the subfloor.

The exterior features an **Arctic Tern window** and a roof-mounted 500W solar array. Two **Maxxair roof fans** provide ventilation and airflow. ## 📡 Cabin
